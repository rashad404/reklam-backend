# Reklam.biz copy and design specificity

Reference: /Users/macmini/projects/azdoc/docs/how-to-detect-ai-generated-ui-ux.md and modern-startup-website-design.md. Read as a research synthesis about perceived sameness, weak contextual judgment and incomplete execution, not an authorship detector.

## Review

The current Reklam public pages repeat three icon benefits, two rounded audience panels, three numbered benefits and a dark closing CTA. Multiple captions restate the platform's purpose. Several polished sentences could describe almost any advertising service. The ad display has unnecessary frames and a duplicate wordmark. These are specific weaknesses to fix; changing fonts or colors alone will not address them.

## Direction

The visitor wants to advertise an offer or earn from a website's available space. Keep the existing brand and working ad formats. Be direct through plain local wording; commercially useful through concrete placement, budget and result information; restrained through fewer repeated blocks and decorative icons.

Keep the hero as the dominant typographic moment. Use an open advertiser section with a short definition list, a separate publisher section describing actual placement choices, useful questions in native disclosures, and a compact final action. The supported ad dimensions provide the product-specific visual detail. Do not add stock faces, invented customer stories, network size claims or fake analytics to make the site appear human.
