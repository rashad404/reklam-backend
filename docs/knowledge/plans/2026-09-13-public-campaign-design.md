# Public campaign design

## Reference and diagnosis

Reviewed https://smartbee.az/az in a rendered desktop browser, including scrolling through its page. Its strong type hierarchy, product illustrations and contrasting sections give it a more developed visual identity. Treat it as a quality reference, not a layout or asset source. No Smartbee assets, copy, statistics, partner logos or visual components are reused.

The Reklam.biz opening repeated the commission promise in two text-heavy columns inside one dark rectangle. The publisher page repeated the offer again immediately below. Preserve the owner's approved headline and six-month offer as the first section, but separate the main action from the financial explanation.

## Direction

Direct: a single large headline and prominent website join action. Confident: a large white 0% on a Reklam.biz red poster. Practical: readable eligibility/fee terms, a secondary advertiser route and a working ad-format gallery. Use existing Manrope with more deliberate sizing, an open neutral canvas and compact graphite closing panel. The oversized commission figure is the signature detail and represents the actual current offer.

Keep publisher acquisition first. Follow with advertising format interaction, grounded advertiser information, FAQ and final action. On the publisher page, the section after the offer explains format choice instead of repeating the commission pitch. Preserve AZ/EN/RU, dark mode, metadata and account behavior.

## Delivery

Implement in shared public components and scoped campaign-design styles. Keep compact authenticated offer presentation unchanged. Verify first-screen actions, 320px/390px/desktop layouts, all formats and attribution, translated pages, both themes, accessibility and terms links. Deploy the frontend with prior release retained; no backend changes.

The owner confirmed 30% commission after the six-month 0% period. This remains the deployed rule and the published terms.
