# Six-month publisher acquisition offer

User-authorized offer: for a publisher's first six months, Reklam.biz takes no platform share of advertising revenue generated on that publisher's website. Actual payment-processing fees and applicable taxes remain separate. Do not describe this as all advertisers' money or guaranteed earnings. Do not claim unknown payment fees will be small.

## Commercial treatment

Six calendar months start at first site approval, so setup time does not consume the offer. Existing approved launch partners receive six months from this offer's deployment. Store immutable start/end dates; reapproval, pauses and edits do not restart the clock. No retroactive earnings changes. Preserve the existing 30% standard rate after expiry unless the owner chooses another rate; an optional clarification was requested. Disclose this existing rate in detailed terms.

## Placement

Homepage hero link and publisher section, publisher landing hero and FAQ, publisher signup/site page, and publisher overview. Keep the external-fee/tax exception adjacent to the main offer and link to localized terms. Show actual expiry in authenticated screens and the actual standard percentage after expiry. Preserve the ad gallery attribution fix.

## Implementation scope

This request explicitly authorizes the promotion's revenue-share rule despite the earlier exclusion of payment implementation. Add publisher offer dates, enroll on first approval, and use a cached standard commission setting in CPC and CPM credit calculations. Do not implement checkout, withdrawals, tax rates, processors, settlement or broad ledger repairs. Existing financial reconciliation limitations remain; this change does not certify them.

## Verification and deployment

Test calendar-month boundaries, reapproval, expiry, cached commission configuration, CPC/CPM crediting and deduplication. Verify new/current/expired offer UI, all three languages, small screens, accessibility, terms links and current journeys. Back up publisher rows before the additive migration.
